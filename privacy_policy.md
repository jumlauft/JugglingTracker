## Privacy Policy

**Privacy Policy for Juggling Tracker (Garmin Watch App)**

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

The Juggling Tracker watch app is designed with your privacy in mind.

1. **Data Collection**: The App uses your watch's accelerometer sensor to detect juggling catches. This motion data is processed in real-time on the device. In Record mode, the raw accelerometer samples of a run are kept on the watch until they are sent to your phone, together with the catch count you confirm.
2. **Personal Information**: The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information (such as your name, email, location, or health metrics like heart rate).
3. **Data Storage and Transmission**: 
    - Juggling session results (ball count, catch counts, run durations and the Regularity score) are held on your watch only until they are sent. Nothing is kept on the watch after the app closes.
    - When connected to a smartphone with the Juggling Tracker Android app installed, session results and Record mode runs are transmitted via Bluetooth, through the Garmin Connect app, to your phone for persistence and visualization.
4. **Third-Party Services**: The watch app does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services.
5. **No Cloud Storage**: The watch app does not upload your data to any external servers or cloud services. All data stays within your local watch-and-phone ecosystem.

1. **Data Collection**: The App uses your watch's accelerometer sensor to detect juggling catches. This motion data is processed in real-time on the device. In Record mode, the raw accelerometer samples of a run are kept on the watch until they are sent to your phone, together with the catch count you confirm.

**Privacy Policy for Juggling Tracker (Android App)**

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

The Juggling Tracker Android app is designed with your privacy in mind.

1. **Data Collection**: The App uses your phone's accelerometer sensor to detect juggling catches when you start a phone session. This motion data is processed in real-time on the device.
2. **Personal Information**: The App does not ask for your email, location, or health metrics like heart rate. If you enter a juggler name when exporting Record mode recordings, it is stored on your phone and written into the exported recording files.
3. **Data Storage and Transmission**: 
    - Juggling session results (catch counts and durations) and Record mode recordings are stored locally on your phone.
    - They leave your phone only when you export or share them yourself (for example as a CSV file or an emailed zip of recordings), to the destination you choose.
4. **Third-Party Services**: The App uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. These receive usage events (for example that a session was imported or a phone session started, with its ball count, number of runs and total catches), crash reports, and device information such as the device model, Android version and an app instance identifier. This is on by default and can be turned off under Settings > Privacy > "Share app activity and crash reports". Google's privacy policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
5. **Bluetooth and Location**: The App uses Bluetooth permissions to communicate with Garmin watches. On Android 11 and older, Android requires the Location permission for Bluetooth; the App does not read or store your location.
6. **No Cloud Storage**: Apart from the Firebase services above, the App does not upload your data to any external servers. Your juggling history stays on your phone unless you export it.
