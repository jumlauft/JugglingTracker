## Privacy Policy

**Privacy Policy for Juggling Tracker (Garmin Watch App)**

Last Updated: June 10, 2026

The Juggling Tracker watch app is designed with your privacy in mind.

1. **Data Collection**: The App uses your watch's accelerometer sensor to detect juggling catches. This motion data is processed in real-time on the device.
2. **Personal Information**: The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information (such as your name, email, location, or health metrics like heart rate).
3. **Data Storage and Transmission**: 
    - Juggling session results (catch counts and durations) are stored locally on your watch.
    - When connected to a smartphone with the Juggling Tracker Android app installed, these session results are transmitted via Bluetooth to your phone for persistence and visualization.
4. **Third-Party Services**: The watch app does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services.
5. **No Cloud Storage**: The watch app does not upload your data to any external servers or cloud services. All data stays within your local watch-and-phone ecosystem.
